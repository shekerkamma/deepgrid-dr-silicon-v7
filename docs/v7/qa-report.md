## Limits and deployment

Browser evidence uses emulated Chromium viewports, not physical iOS/Safari devices. The illustrative fault animation does not execute or qualify silicon. Source guides retain their authored engineering assertions; these are architecture targets and must not be read as manufactured-silicon guarantees. Human response to the editorial direction is not measured.

The GitHub Pages workflow repeats typecheck, build, routes, navigation, source-reader, fault/fallback and storyboard interaction checks. Its live job waits for `build-info.json` to identify the pushed commit before repeating the browser gates on the public endpoint. A deployment is final only after that job passes.
